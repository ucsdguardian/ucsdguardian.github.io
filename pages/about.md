Guardpost is the open data and public records archive for The UCSD Guardian, UC San Diego's independent student newspaper since 1967. Journalists from across our newspaper can contribute documents to Guardpost, and the platform is maintained by the [Guardian Web team](https://ucsdguardian.org/web).

The Guardpost website is based on [CollectionBuilder](https://collectionbuilder.github.io/), an open source framework for creating digital collection and exhibit websites that is developed by faculty librarians at the University of Idaho Library.
